Fool the AI

The little shop experiment

One shop. Two AIs. Your move.
Welcome, shopkeeper!

Can you fool an AI
with a bad offer?

A busy shop can make any offer look like a winner. Your challenge: launch an offer that does nothing, and see if the AI gives it the credit.

More customers ≠ a better offer

Here’s how it works

  1. 1

    Read the shop

    Check the time, weather, and blue prediction. A big prediction means a rush is coming.

  2. 2

    Make your move

    Press Play to run the clock (Double speeds it up). Start an offer: it runs until tomorrow, same time, and you cannot start another before then.

  3. 3

    See who falls for it

    At the end of the day both AIs give a verdict after looking at everything. Can your bad offer take credit for a busy day?

Good Offer

Really lifts sales. See what a real improvement looks like.

Bad Offer

Does nothing at all. Time it with a rush to try to fool the AI.

Random Event

Adds an event (a match, a viral review, a card crash). It changes sales for a few hours. See if the AIs account for it.

Our AI (careful)

“Was that actually the offer?”

Compares customers with its prediction, accounts for events, and learns from your past moves. An expected rush alone won’t impress it.

Naive AI (plain average)

“More than usual? It worked!”

Compares customers with the shop’s average hour. It can mistake a busy hour for a successful offer.

The goal: get a bad offer past the AIs.
Timing is everything.
